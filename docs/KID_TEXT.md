## Challenge introductions

| Challenge | Text |
|---|---|
| L Life Support | "The machine that makes oxygen is slow. The crew has some backup air, but not much. How will you keep everyone breathing?" |
| R Radiation | "A solar storm is coming at the end of Sol 2. Radiation can hurt the crew. Do you shelter, shield, or keep working?" |
| W Power | "The habitat does not have enough power for everything. Heaters, air machines, radios and the science lab all want electricity. Who goes first?" |
| G Greenhouse | "Fresh plants can give the crew food and air. But plants need water, power and time. Will you plant now?" |
| M Earth | "A warning light came on. You sent a message to Earth, but the reply will take a long time. What will the crew do while they wait?" |

## Classroom questions (pick 2 for each debrief)

1. Why can't astronauts wait for Earth to answer?
2. When is it smart to keep working in a storm? When is it not?
3. What did you give up to protect the crew?
4. Why does a greenhouse need a plan on Sol 1?
5. What would you do differently on a second try?
